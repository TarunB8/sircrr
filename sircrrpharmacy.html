College Vision

To be a leader in Pharmacy education, research and training for future academic and practicing pharmacists with the ultimate goal of providing pharmaceutical education and building competencies to deliver healthcare services to society.

College Mission

Sir C. R. Reddy College of Pharmaceutical Sciences was established to impart high-quality education, training and research in Pharmacy and to prepare pharmacy professionals to meet societal needs. The institution strives to prepare graduates capable of delivering value-based, patient-centered care through excellence in teaching, research, service and pharmacy practice.

Opportunities in Pharmacy

Pharmacy offers a wide range of specializations and rewarding career opportunities in scientific research, healthcare services, teaching and business. The pharmaceutical industry employs pharmacy graduates in production, quality control, research and development, and formulation.


Higher studies such as M.Pharm and Ph.D broaden career prospects in teaching, research institutions and industry. Pharmacy graduates are also employed in hospital pharmacy departments. Excellent opportunities exist abroad in countries like the USA, Germany, Australia and Middle Eastern nations.


Pharm D (Doctor of Pharmacy) is a six-year professional degree recognized by western countries, enabling graduates to work as clinical and hospital pharmacists.
