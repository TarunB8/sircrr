Dr. Alluri Indra Kumar

President

Dr. Alluri Indra Kumar stands at the helm of Sir C.R. Reddy Educational Institutions as President, continuing the proud legacy established by visionary educators and philanthropists. With a deep commitment to educational excellence and the holistic development of students, Dr. Kumar brings both scholarly acumen and administrative expertise to this esteemed position.

Under his visionary leadership, the institution continues to uphold the founding principles of providing quality education that transforms lives and fosters intellectual growth. Dr. Kumar is dedicated to advancing the academic standards across all educational verticals of the institution, from primary education through postgraduate and doctoral programs.

His tenure as President is marked by a commitment to fostering an inclusive educational environment where students from diverse socioeconomic backgrounds can access world-class education. He actively champions the adoption of modern pedagogical approaches while preserving the institutional values that have made Sir C.R. Reddy Institutions a beacon of academic excellence in Andhra Pradesh.

Dr. Kumar's strategic vision encompasses the expansion of professional and technical programs, the enhancement of infrastructure facilities, and the cultivation of partnerships with national and international academic institutions. His leadership ensures that the institution remains at the forefront of educational innovation while maintaining steadfast commitment to the educational empowerment of students.

Through his administrative oversight and intellectual guidance, Dr. Alluri Indra Kumar embodies the spirit of educational leadership, ensuring that Sir C.R. Reddy Educational Institutions continues to shape generations of enlightened citizens who contribute meaningfully to society and the nation.
