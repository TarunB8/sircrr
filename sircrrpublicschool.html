About Sir C.R. Reddy Public School

Sir C.R. Reddy Public School, located on G.N.T. Road, Santhi Nagar, Eluru, Andhra Pradesh, is a premier CBSE affiliated institution (Affiliation No. 130072), dedicated to academic excellence and holistic child development.


Established in 1984, the school is managed by the prestigious Sir C R Reddy Educational Institutions’ Governing Body, a name synonymous with quality education in Andhra Pradesh.


The school offers a nurturing learning environment from Nursery to Class XII, empowering students with knowledge, values, and essential 21st-century skills.
