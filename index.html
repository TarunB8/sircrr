BRIEF HISTORY

From a single academic initiative in 1945 to a comprehensive educational ecosystem, Sir C.R. Reddy Educational Institutions has grown into a "Mini-University" serving thousands of students across multiple disciplines and educational levels.

Foundation & Growth

In the 1940s, there was a long-felt need for quality higher education in West Godavari District. Late Sri Rao Saheb Damaraju Venkata Rao initiated the establishment of the college in 1944 with the support of philanthropic citizens.

The college was inaugurated on 4th July 1945 by Sir Cattamanchi Ramalinga Reddy and named "Sir C.R. Reddy College".

1945 – 1970
First Grade status in 1951, Pre-University course in 1957, Degree programme in 1958, Intermediate in 1969, and PG Commerce in 1971.
1971 – 1987
PG courses in Politics, Economics, and English; Women’s Degree College in 1983; Polytechnic College in 1987.
1988 – 2005
Engineering College (1988), Pharmacy (1993), Nursing (2000), Autonomous status (2001), expansion of professional education.
2006 – Present
NAAC 'A' Grade, CPE status by UGC, 10+ institutions, 15,000+ students, multi-disciplinary academic ecosystem.
Legacy & Recognition
Recognized as a premier educational hub in Andhra Pradesh with modern infrastructure, strong alumni network, and commitment to quality education.
