About Sir C Ramalinga Reddy Polytechnic

Sir C Ramalinga Reddy Polytechnic is the first private Polytechnic College in Andhra Pradesh sanctioned and recognized by AICTE and permanently affiliated to SBTET since inception. Established in 1980, the institution has four decades of excellence in delivering quality technical education.


Located near Vatluru Flyover, Eluru, on the Chennai–Howrah highway and railway route, the college is easily accessible from all parts of the country. Spread across 11.19 acres, the green and serene campus provides a stimulating environment for students and staff alike.
